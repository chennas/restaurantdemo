Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended, especially for dinner and weekends. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability.

Do you offer parking?

Yes, we have validated parking available. Street parking and public parking lots are also nearby.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and cash. Contactless payments are also available.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We offer vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and allergy-friendly options. Please inform us when ordering.
